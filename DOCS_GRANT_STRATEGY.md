# Non-Dilutive Public Funding Strategy: SBIR/STTR Targets

To fund early physical hardware validation testing without diluting company equity, we will actively target United States federal agency **Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR)** grants.

## 1. Primary Agency Opportunities

### A. Department of Defense (DoD) — AFWERX / DARPA
*   **Focus Area:** Trusted Autonomy, Resilient Drone Swarm Architectures, and Software Self-Healing Environments.
*   **Solicitation Positioning:** Market the project as a firmware-level protection mechanism against electronic warfare/cyber exploitation capabilities, ensuring asset return despite navigation software loss.
*   **Target:** SBIR Phase I (\$150k feasibility award) moving into Phase II (\$1.2M execution grant).

### B. National Science Foundation (NSF) — Digital and Information Technologies
*   **Focus Area:** Highly Reliable Distributed Compute Systems and Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS).
*   **Solicitation Positioning:** Advance foundational computer architecture science by commercializing mathematically bounded homeostatic balance controllers.
